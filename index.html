Heart Attack Risk Prediction

A complete logistic regression pipeline on 8,763 patient records from Kaggle: blood-pressure feature engineering, stratified splitting, leak-free scaling, and balanced class weights. Privacy

What happens to your details.

Short version: I keep what you type in the contact form so I can reply to you, and nothing else. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing sold to anyone.

What I keep

Your name and email address, because I need them to write back.

Your phone number and anything you write about yourself, if you choose to fill those in. Both are optional and the form works without them.

Why

To answer you. That is the only reason.

I do not send newsletters or marketing. You get one confirmation when you submit the form, and then only a direct reply from me.

Where it lives

In a database hosted by Neon on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.

Two other services touch it: Resend delivers the email, and Vercel runs the site. Neither uses your details for anything of their own.

I am the only person with access.

Getting rid of it

Every email I send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and your row is deleted outright — name, phone and message included. Not hidden or flagged, actually removed.

You can also just email me and I will do it by hand.

I keep your details until you ask me to remove them. If you would like to know what I hold on you, ask and I will tell you. Written in plain language rather than legal language — if something here is unclear, email me and I will explain it properly.
