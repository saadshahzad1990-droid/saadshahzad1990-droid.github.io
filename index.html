Heart attack risk model

Heart Attack Risk Prediction

A complete logistic regression pipeline on 8,763 patient records from Kaggle: blood-pressure feature engineering, stratified splitting, leak-free scaling, and balanced class weights. Exploration flagged the dataset as synthetic (every feature correlation under 0.02), so the honest finding is methodological: a correct pipeline can't invent signal that isn't there.
